## Tuition fee refunds in the case of a termination of enrollment request

If you terminate your enrollment at TU/e, you may be entitled to a tuition fee refund. The tuition fees for the remaining months will be refunded to the account number you provide in Studielink no later than ten weeks after the confirmation of your termination of enrollment. This also takes into account any installments still to be collected in the case of payment in installments. You are entitled to a refund of one twelfth of the tuition fees for each month remaining in the academic year following the termination of enrollment.

If your enrollment is terminated effective as of July 1 or August 1, you cannot claim a tuition refund for those months.

For more information on the termination of enrollment and tuition fee refunds, see the [education guide](https://educationguide.tue.nl/practical-info/student-administration/termination-of-enrollment).

## Scholarships

### **Undergraduate students (Bachelor)**

TU/e does not offer scholarships for bachelor’s students.

### **Postgraduate students (Master)**

TU/e offers an exclusive **Scholarship for Excellence** for highly talented international students who want to help shape the future of microchip technology. This scholarship is available for applicants to selected Master’s programs in the Departments of **Applied Physics**, **Electrical Engineering**, **Mechanical Engineering**, and **Mathematics & Computer Science**. Apply before February 1st, 2026, 23:59 CET and meet the scholarship requirements if you would like to start in September 2026.

Learn more about the Scholarship for Excellence, the selected Master’s programs and find out [here](/en/education/become-a-tue-student/scholarships-and-grants/tue-scholarship-for-excellence-shaping-the-future-of-microchip-innovation) if you are eligble.

## Please note

- When applying for any scholarship, make sure you provide the scholarship provider with the **full and correct TU/e [tuition fee.](/en/education/become-a-tue-student/tuition-fees-and-other-study-costs/tuition-fee)  
 -**If your scholarship does not cover the entire tuition fee, you are personally responsible for paying the **remaining amount** before you can be enrolled at TU/e.

## Student grants or student travel product

As an international student, you may be eligible for financial support from the Dutch government. This can include a grant, a loan, and/or a student travel product. Your eligibility depends on factors such as your nationality, your residence status and length of your stay.  
 [**Check the website of DUO**](https://www.duo.nl/particulier/international-visitor/) if you qualify for student finance and/or a student travel product.

## Find other scholarships and funding opportunities

You can explore other scholarships and funding opportunities offered by external organizations. Useful resources include:

* [Study in NL Website](https://www.studyinnl.org/finances) – a database with scholarships offered by various organizations.
* [European Commission Scholarship Portal](https://commission.europa.eu/education/study-or-teach-abroad/scholarships-and-student-finance_en) – a platform of European funding and scholarships opportunities.
* [Find Scholarships to Fund Your Study Abroad | Mastersportal](https://www.mastersportal.com/scholarships/)
* Several other international organizations may offer financial support for studying abroad. [**UNESCO**](http://www.unesco.org/), [**UNDP**](http://www.undp.org/), [**Unido**](http://www.unido.org/), [**the World Bank**](http://www.worldbank.org/), [**OECD**](http://www.oecd.org/), [**the**](http://www.adb.org/) [**Asian Development Bank**](http://www.adb.org/) and the [**the European Union**](http://europa.eu/). They may have a local office in your country, or you can reach out to their main office.

## Tuition fees and other study costs

Studying involves different kinds of university expenses. There are for example the costs of tuition fees, books and other study materials, a laptop and the costs of renting a room. On the other hand you might be eligible for student grants. Below you can read more about the different costs that might arise.

## Amounts of different fees

* [### Application fee

  Students with a foreign education pay an application fee of € 100 for each application.

  Read more](/en/education/become-a-tue-student/tuition-fees-and-other-study-costs/application-fee)
* [### Tuition fee

  Different fees per student and the requirements

  Read more](/en/education/become-a-tue-student/tuition-fees-and-other-study-costs/tuition-fee)
* [### Guarantee fee

  Students with a Non EEA nationality who have been admitted to a TU/e Bachelor's or Master's program based on a non-Dutch previous education, pay a guarantee fee in advance to confirm that they will enroll at the TU/e.

  Read more](/en/education/become-a-tue-student/tuition-fees-and-other-study-costs/guarantee-fee)
* [### Proof of financial means

  For the application of your residence permit you need to prove to the IND that you have sufficient financial means to fund your studies and to live in the Netherlands during your studies.

  Read more](/en/education/become-a-tue-student/tuition-fees-and-other-study-costs/proof-of-financial-means)

***Living expenses***  
 *Per year a TU/e student needs a budget of at least €10,000-€12,000 for rent, insurance, living expenses, books and local transportation. This is a minimum budget and is in addition to the tuition fee and any expenses incurred travelling from your home country to Eindhoven.*  
 *Rent can vary between**€400 for a room and €700 for a studio.*

1. [Check your admission requirements](#enrollment-232252-tabpanel-1)

   You are eligible for admission if you hold the high school diploma Bang Tot Nghiep Pho Thong Trung Hoc,  obtained at a high school for the gifted with Mathematics and, if required by the program, Physics and/or Chemistry as mandatory final examination subjects.  
    You must obtain an overall GPA of at least 75% and a score of at least 75% for Mathematics (and for Physics and Chemistry, if required).

   We can only assess your previous education when we have received a full application package and online application.

   Check the links for more information about eligibility with an [European Baccalaureate, International Baccalaureate, British GCE A levels or American High School](/en/education/become-a-tue-student/admission-and-enrollment/eligibility).

4. [Transfer the application fee](#enrollment-232252-tabpanel-4)

   An application fee of € 100 is required per application. The fee is non-refundable. Proof that this fee has been transferred must be uploaded in the online application form.

   Students living in or with the nationality of the following countries: Cuba, Iran, North-Korea, Russia, Sudan or Syria must NOT transfer the application fee, as TU/e is obliged to comply with regulations due to sanctions regarding these countries. Instead, please provide the requested details in the OSIRIS application form.

   Once you submit your application via Studielink, you will receive an email from TU/e usually within 2 days. This email contains:

   * Your login details for our system OSIRIS
   * An invitation to complete the TU/e application form

   If you do not receive this email within 2 days, please contact the International Office at [io@remove-this.tue.nl](#) and mention you haven’t received the OSIRIS application request.

   Your application must be complete by 15 January, 23.59 CET. We strongly recommend submitting your application as early as possible, ideally at least one month before the deadline. This gives us time to review your documents and notify you if anything is missing – so you can still meet the deadline.

7. [Announcement ranking numbers 15 April](#enrollment-232252-tabpanel-7)

   On 15 April your ranking number will be announced via Studielink.

   If your ranking number falls within the maximum number, you will be offered a place on the program. You then have 14 days to accept this place.

   Chances are high that you will be offered a place if you have a ranking number higher than the maximum. One of the reasons is that some candidates have registered for several bachelor's programs so it is possible places are pushed forward. We will offer places until August 15th if the maximum available places has not been reached.

9. [Send in your diploma and transcript for verifying your previous education](#enrollment-232252-tabpanel-9)

   As part of the enrollment process, TU/e is required to verify your previous education, which is one of the formal enrollment criteria. If you halve already completed the education on which your admission is based, you will be asked to provide the necessary official documents, such as certified copies of your degree or verification details through your institution’s database, shortly after you receive our admissions decision. If you are still completing the previous education, you will be asked to provide these documents or details, after graduation. You will receive this request automatically, based on your personal situation and timeline.

   Please make sure to submit the requested documents or verification details as soon as possible after receiving our instructions. This will allow us to verify your credentials and move you one step closer to officially becoming a TU/e student.

   **Ways to submit your documents or verification details**  
    Below are the ways you can submit your documents. You will receive detailed instructions as part of the follow-up procedure, which will be sent to you automatically. Please wait until you receive this invitation before sending any documents.

   *Online verification*  
    If your university, school or college offers an online verification service, we ask you to provide us with the login details. You can supply us with these details in the follow-up procedure.  
      
    *E-mail from your university or school*  
    Your university, school or college can email us your diploma and transcript and possible certificates. They can email it to [io.documents@tue.nl](https://www.tue.nl/en/education/become-a-tue-student/admission-and-enrollment/programtype/master-program/program/innovation-sciences/country/botswana-1) and provide your full name, TU/e student number in the subject.

   *Certified copies by post*  
    You can send us a certified copy of your degree and transcript and possible certificates from your previous education.

   Certified copies are copies with an original stamp to verify that the copies are true copies of the original documents. This official stamp may be from one of the following institutions:

   * the educational institution from where you gained the diploma
   * an embassy
   * a solicitor or notary
   * a local town or city council.

   Never send your original documents.

   *Official statement by post*  
    If you have not yet received your diploma and official academic transcript, we require an official statement from your university, school or college stating you have completed your previous education. This statement must be an original document (not a copy) on the official headed paper of the institution you have obtained the diploma or certificate and must contain an overview of your grades per subject. The statement is to be in the English language.

   Important notes when sending us documents by post:

   * If your diploma and transcript are not available in English or Dutch, please include a certified/authenticated translation
   * Make sure to send us all your documents at one time, in one envelope. Please do not send your documents at different times, in different envelopes.
   * Make sure to always include your personal student number.
   * You can send the certified copies of your diploma and transcript or the official statement to:

   Eindhoven University of Technology  
    Education and Student Affairs  
    Admission Office MF 1.300  
    PO Box 513  
    5600 MB Eindhoven

10. [Let us know your visa- and residence permit situation (non-EEA only)](#enrollment-232252-tabpanel-10)

    Please find detailed information on our [Immigration, bank account and insurance](/en/education/become-a-tue-student/visa-residence-permit) website.

    Most students with a [non-EEA](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/eu-eea-efta-schengen-countries) nationality who want to stay in the Netherlands for more than 90 days need an entry visa (MVV) and/or residence permit (VVR). Your nationality determines the immigration arrangements you need to take before coming to study at TU/e. To check if you need a visa and/or residence permit, you should check the [Nuffic website.](https://www.nuffic.nl/en)

    **What is the EEA?**

    EEA stands for the *European Economic Area*, which includes all EU countries plus Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway. Although Switzerland is not part of the EEA, it is often treated similarly for tuition and admission purposes. If you do not hold the nationality of an EEA-country or Switzerland, you are considered a non-EEA student.

    **Immigration procedure**

    From April onwards, all admitted non-EEA students will receive a visa/residence permit application form by email. This form will ask you to indicate your residence situation and guide you through the necessary steps. This will help us determine which immigration arrangements apply to you. The deadline for submitting this form, and making the required payments, if applicable (see ‘Transfer payments’), is 1 June, 23.59 CET.

    The Dutch Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) allows limited flexibility, so it’s crucial to submit your admissions application form, and visa/residence permit application form, as early as possible. Early submissions helps us start your immigration process on time and brings you one step closer to a complete enrollment.

    Please regularly check your inbox – including spam – from April onwards for the visa/residence permit application form.

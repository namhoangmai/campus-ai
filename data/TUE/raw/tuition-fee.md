## Tuition fees

How much you pay in tuition fees depends on your personal situation. Different fees apply: the statutory fee, the institutional fee and other fees. At TU/e, the same fees apply to part-time students as to full-time students.

## Statutory fee

If you have EU/EEA, Swiss or Surinamese nationality and have not previously obtained a comparable degree in Dutch higher education, you pay the statutory fee. More information about the applicable conditions can be found on the [DUO website](https://duo.nl/particulier/tuition-fees.jsp).

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | 2026-2027 | 2027-2028 |
| Statutory fee | € 2,694 | t.b.a. |

## Institutional fee

If you do not have EU/EEA, Swiss or Surinamese nationality or if you have previously obtained a comparable degree in Dutch higher education, you will pay the institutional fee. When you start at TU/e, you pay a one-time **[deposit](/en/education/become-a-tue-student/tuition-fees-and-other-study-costs/guarantee-fee)**. The tuition fee for the first year is included in the deposit. More information about the applicable conditions can be found on the [DUO website](https://duo.nl/particulier/tuition-fees.jsp).

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | 2026-2027 | 2027-2028 |
| Institutional fee for bachelor’s students | € 18,600 | € 22,400 |
| Institutional fee for master’s students | € 21,700 | € 22,400 |

**Transitional arrangements:**

1. Non-EEA students who started their bachelor program academy year 2024-2025 (or earlier), pay a lower institutional tuition fee till 31 Augustus 2028. This lower rate is determined yearly. N.B. the lower institutional tuition fee 2026-2027 amounted to € 14.200,-  
   
 2. Non-EEA students who started their master program academy year 2024-2025 (or earlier), pay a lower institutional tuition fee till 31 Augustus 2027. N.B. the lower institutional tuition fee 2026-2027 amounted to € 20.500,-

The lower tuition fee rate applies only for one bachelor's program or for one master's program.

3. Students with the EU/EEA, Swiss, or Surinamese nationality who have previously obtained a comparable degree in Dutch higher education pay an adjusted institutional tuition fee. For more information about this tuition fee, please contact TU/e via the [contact form](#c256742) below on the webpage.

## Fee for pre-master's students

When you enroll on a pre-master’s program, the program itself determines the total number of credits of your pre-master’s program. For HBO pre-university education, this is 30 by default. The number of credits determines the fee that you pay: number of credits \* 1/60 statutory fee (A).

You pay your fee in a lump sum when you start your pre-master’s program. Refunds are not possible.

Halfway through the year, any exemptions or additional courses will be settled. Upon completion of your pre-master’s program, you will enroll on a master’s program and pay and you pay the applicable rate depending on your situation. The master rate you have to pay can be found in the statutory fee table or the institutional fee table.
